Title: SourceCoverOptimizer — Consensus-Grounded Minimum Source Bundles

Description:

SourceCoverOptimizer is a reusable GenLayer minimum-documentation-cover primitive. An immutable pack fixes reading goals, candidate RFCs and time bounds. Leader and validators independently fetch full RFC Editor bodies, hash raw bytes and semantically derive each requirement-coverage row. Exact agreement on bindings, hashes, byte costs, coverage and decisions is required. An exact subset solver selects the minimum-byte bundle covering every goal; the model cannot supply winners or costs. Missing, stale, invalid or ambiguous rows yield INCOMPLETE; complete but insufficient coverage yields UNCOVERED. Anyone can observe and close after all rows finish or the deadline. Pack-bound reports and terminal solutions prevent replay and resampling. No graph or certificate. StudioNet selected RFCs 2606 and 5737, excluded irrelevant RFC 2119, and recorded an uncovered case. Source matches GitHub. Coverage concerns document contents, not real-world truth. 29 mocked direct tests pass.
